Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CampusConnect

Is CampusConnect free for students? ▼
Yes! CampusConnect is completely free for students. Create your account, build your profile, and apply to unlimited jobs at no cost.
Will I receive email notifications for every update? ▼
Absolutely. Every important action — registration, application submitted, shortlisted, interview scheduled, selected or rejected — triggers an instant email to your inbox. No need to keep checking the platform.
How do companies post jobs? ▼
Companies register, complete their profile, and can post a job in under 2 minutes using our simple job form. Each posting is reviewed before going live to students.
Can the university admin see all placements? ▼
Yes. The admin panel gives university placement officers full visibility — student applications, company activity, placement rates, branch-wise data, and exportable reports.
What happens after I apply for a job? ▼
You can track your application status in real-time from your dashboard. You'll also receive email notifications at every stage — shortlisted, interview scheduled, final decision.
Is my data secure on CampusConnect? ▼
Yes. All passwords are encrypted, authentication uses secure JWT tokens, and your personal data is never shared with third parties without your consent.
